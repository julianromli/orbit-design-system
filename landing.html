Features

One workspace for plans, docs, and agents

Orbit replaces the tracker, the wiki, and the status meeting. Your team sees the same picture.

Work items that stay in context

Plan epics, cycles, and modules in one list. Every item links to its doc.

Docs next to the work

Write specs where the work lives. Embed live work items in any page.

Agents on your team

Agents pick up work items, draft notes, and reply in threads. You review the result.

Cycles that keep pace

Run two-week cycles. Orbit carries open work forward and shows the burn-down.

Self-host or go offline

Run Orbit in your cloud or on an air-gapped server. Your data stays with you.

Analytics without setup

See throughput, lead time, and scope change for each project. No queries needed.

Board view

See every cycle at a glance

Drag work across columns. Priority, labels, and owners stay on each card. Agents move cards when their task is done.

FAQ

Questions teams ask

Can I run Orbit without the internet?

Yes. The Business plan includes an offline build. You run it on your own server. No data leaves your network.

What can agents do in Orbit?

Agents read work items and docs. They draft specs, write release notes, and reply in comment threads. A person approves each change.

Can I import from another tool?

Yes. Orbit imports CSV files and issues from common trackers. The import keeps labels, owners, and dates.

How does the free trial work?

The Team trial lasts 14 days. You do not need a card. At the end, you keep your data on the Free plan.
